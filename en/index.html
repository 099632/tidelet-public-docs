Your records, on your device

Tidelet helps you record cycle information, view statistics, set local reminders and transfer data manually using complete backups.

Free download, one-time unlock

Choose up to 2 people to edit at a time for free, and change that selection whenever you like. All people’s data is retained. Other people remain available to view, fully back up or delete.

Only editing more than 2 people at a time and sharing an individual person’s images, text, QR codes or profile files require the one-time purchase. Complete ZIP backups and restores, statistics, reminders, widgets, photos, styles and all other existing local features are free.

No subscription. The purchase screen shows the local price supplied by the App Store. Family Sharing is planned; sharing the purchase does not automatically share health data. Availability depends on the final store settings and Apple’s rules.

Health information

Calculations and estimates are uncertain. They are not diagnosis, treatment or a guarantee of contraceptive or fertility reliability. Seek professional medical advice about health concerns. Obtain the relevant permission before recording or sharing another person’s information.
