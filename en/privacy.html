Tidelet privacy policy

Operator: 何致远. Public contact: Tidelet Support · tidelet2026@163.com. This policy applies to Tidelet for Apple devices.

Local information and its use

Tidelet stores nicknames, photos, cycle reference values, period records and settings on your device for local display, calculations, statistics and reminders. No Tidelet account is required. There are no ads. Health records are not uploaded to the developer, and the app does not offer integrated iCloud/CloudKit health synchronization. Calculations and date estimates are for reference, not diagnosis, treatment or a guarantee of contraceptive or fertility reliability. Obtain the relevant permission before recording or sharing another person’s information.

Photos, camera, notifications and widgets

You choose and confirm a cropped photo before it is saved; canceling preserves the existing photo. Camera access is for scanning you initiate. You may refuse it and import manually. Local reminders need system notification permission and hide names and cycle details by default; you can change this setting. Widgets read a derived display snapshot through the local App Group. Notifications and widgets may still expose information to people nearby, so check the app and system display settings.

Backups, sharing and imports

Complete ZIP backups include all retained profiles, records, photos, trash and transferable settings, and are free. Imports retain the complete data set. Choose up to 2 people to edit at a time for free, with other people read-only; viewing, complete backups and deletion remain available. Only editing more people and per-person sharing require a one-time purchase. Changes to purchase access do not delete local data, and backups do not transfer purchase access.

Files, text, images and QR codes may contain sensitive health data. Compression and encoding are not encryption. Check the scope, destination and recipient. Copies you explicitly send to a file provider, mail or messaging service, or recipient are handled by that service or recipient. Tidelet cannot retract delivered copies, and deleting local data does not delete them. Keep and protect important backups yourself.

Retention, trash and deletion

Data remains on the device until you change or delete it, or the app processes expired trash. You can restore trash within 30 times 24 hours. Expiry is processed during app cleanup, not at a guaranteed instant while the app is not running. Permanent deletion removes app-managed data and photos no longer referenced, without promising secure flash erasure or removal of original library photos, older backups or external copies. Private local files use system file protection and are marked for exclusion from system backups. These settings do not protect unencrypted copies you explicitly export.

Apple purchases and Family Sharing

Apple’s App Store/StoreKit handles purchases, restoration and entitlement checks, which may require an internet connection and an Apple account. The app checks product identifiers, transaction verification, ownership and revocation status locally to provide purchase access. It does not send health profiles to the payment service or request payment-card information. Apple processes payments, refunds and purchase sharing under its service rules. Family Sharing shares purchase access, not health data.

Voluntary support emails

If you choose to send a support email, your mail service and the support team receive your sending address, message and chosen attachments. The default draft includes no health records, photos or logs. Provide the version, device, reproduction steps and synthetic examples, and redact health details from screenshots. Do not send identity documents, passwords, verification codes or payment-card information.

The support address uses the 163 email service to transmit and retain mail. The operator retains support content only to handle requests, necessary follow-up and applicable obligations, not for advertising. Support is handled within available capacity, without a fixed response-time promise. You may use the same address to request deletion of emails or attachments you sent. After checking the request, the operator will address copies under the operator’s control. Legal retention requirements or provider backups may impose limits. The operator cannot delete independent copies in your own mailbox, with providers or with other recipients.

Website and external links

This website uses GitHub Pages static hosting. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The pages add no advertising, behavioral analytics, cookie analytics or third-party scripts. External health-information links open in the system browser. Those websites process visits under their own policies; Tidelet does not append your health records to their URLs.

Your choices and contact

You can refuse optional permissions, turn off reminders, manage widgets, make complete backups, change or delete local data and change your free editable selection. Tidelet has no developer-operated account to close. Contact the address above about this policy, support emails or applicable access, correction or deletion requests. The operator will respond according to the circumstances and applicable requirements. You do not need to send local health records to support to delete them.
