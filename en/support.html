Help and support

Tidelet Support · tidelet2026@163.com

Operator: 何致远. Support is handled within available capacity, without a fixed response-time promise. Email providers transmit and retain support mail, which is used only to handle requests, necessary follow-up and applicable obligations. You may request deletion of sent messages or attachments using the same address. The operator can address copies under the operator’s control, not independent copies in your mailbox or with other recipients.

What is free, and what does the unlock include?

Edit up to 2 people at a time for free and change your selection whenever you like. All data is retained; other people are read-only. Only removing the editable-person limit and per-person sharing require a one-time purchase. Complete ZIP backups and restores and all other existing local features are free. There is no subscription. The app shows the local price supplied by Apple.

What if I import more than 2 people?

The import retains all people and records. Choose up to 2 people for free editing. You can still view, fully back up or delete other people’s data. Changes to purchase access do not delete data. Check profiles, photos, dates, records and trash before disposing of the original backup.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the App Store account associated with the purchase and choose “Restore purchases” in the app. Family Sharing also depends on Apple’s account and purchase-sharing rules and the final IAP settings. Pending approval, verification failures, connection problems or a missing entitlement leave free features available and data intact. Apple handles payments and refunds.

Reminders, backups and feedback

Check the app’s reminders, notification permission and Focus modes; the system controls delivery. A reminder does not create a record. Files, text, images and QR codes are not an encrypted vault. Check their scope and recipient, and delete external copies separately. This release does not offer app-integrated iCloud health synchronization.

For feedback, include the app version, device model, OS version, reproduction steps and redacted synthetic screenshots. Do not attach real health backups, photos, passwords, verification codes or payment-card details. An unsent email draft does not mean support received your request.
